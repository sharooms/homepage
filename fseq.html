Performance

Drum Sequencer

Create custom drum loops effortlessly. FSEQ2 offers an intuitive step sequencer interface that makes programming, visualizing, and building your beats fast, fun, and highly visual.

nEw in Version 2
Full Control

Live Mode

Live Mode bypasses FSEQ2's internal playback engine to act as a real-time remote for MIDI clips in Ableton. Any changes to steps, velocity, and timing instantly sync with your Ableton clips and vice versa, enabling combined workflows like humanizing patterns in FSEQ2 while utilizing Ableton's groove pool.

Precision

Select

Edit with surgical precision. Use the select tool to highlight multiple steps or rows, then apply familiar keyboard shortcuts to instantly cut, copy, paste, or duplicate your patterns. You can even use it to apply a random fill to your selection, making complex edits and variations fast and intuitive.

Expression

Velocity

Add dynamic expression to your patterns. Paint velocity curves or fine-tune individual steps to bring your drum grooves to life with human-like feel.

nEw in Version 2
Expression

Timing Expressions

Move steps off the grid while maintaining full visual clarity. Easily keep track of your sequence even when using functions in Live Mode that shift notes away from the grid, such as custom grooves or humanize features.

Workflow

Hotkeys

Speed up your workflow with intuitive keyboard shortcuts. Auto-filling, cutting, copying, and pasting entire rows, alongside powerful mouse scroll-wheel functions for quick adjustments.

Integration

API Integration

Experience deep integration with Ableton Live. The bi-directional API communication ensures that your sequencer and Drum Rack are always perfectly in sync, including automatic selection of Drum Rack pads and chains.

nEw in Version 2
Workflow

DrumKit + Sample Manager

Create, save, and manage custom drum kits with multiple samples per drum pad. Save variations of your favorite combinations to instantly swap sound profiles and keep your library organized.

Sound Design

Sample Swap

Swap loaded samples directly in Simpler via FSEQ2 for maximum RAM efficiency. While the classic, memory-heavy Chain Selector method is still supported as a legacy option, direct hot-swapping inside Simpler is now the first choice.

nEw in Version 2
Inspiration

Dice

Roll the dice for instant creativity. Generate new sample variations on the fly. Decide which pads get rerolled by locking the ones you love, or roll individual pads to fine-tune your kit.

get inspired

Preset Management

Seamlessly save and recall your favorite patterns. Presets are stored as JSON files in a custom folder of your choice,. The integrated preset browser lets you manage your library without leaving the creative workflow. Easily share your custom presets with others and discover new ones to get inspired.

Production

Create

Turn sequences into MIDI clips with a single click. Export patterns directly to Live's Arrangement View and assign custom hotkeys to generate clips on the fly. even when the FSEQ2 interface is closed.

Customization

Manual Mode

Take full control of your sequence names and MIDI notes. Manual mode allows you to bypass Drum Rack integration for custom mappings and unique hardware setups. You can even save your custom configurations as presets, so they are instantly ready to use with your favorite instruments.

Style

Ableton Themes

FSEQ2 changes with the Ableton color theme. This ensures that the interface always fits perfectly into your workspace, regardless of whether you prefer a dark or light theme.

Specifications

System Requirements

Software

Ableton Live Suite (Max for Live is required). Ableton Live 12.4 is required to use all features.
